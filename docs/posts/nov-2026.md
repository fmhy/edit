# Stars Added ⭐

- Starred [ShrubLibrary](https://fmhy.net/mobile#ios-ipas) in iOS iPAs, lots of apps, custom sources and clean UI.

- Starred [calibre](https://fmhy.net/reading#e-reader-tools) in E-Reader Tools, customizable ebook management app with converter, editor, reader, & more.

- Starred [Discogs](https://fmhy.net/audio#tracking-databases) in Music Tracking / Databases, one of the largest, best maintained, and well respected music databases.

- Starred [Toonkotsu](https://fmhy.net/reading#manga) in Manga, nice UI & reader, multi-source, seems to be well liked.

- Starred [The Pudding](https://fmhy.net/misc#interesting) in Interesting Section, high-quality interactive data visualizations and visual essays. 

- Starred [Paster](https://fmhy.net/text-tools#pastebins) in Pastebins, has multi-syntax, markdown support and edit codes.

- Starred [Inkscape](https://fmhy.net/image-tools#painting-drawing) in Painting / Drawing Section, open-source vector graphics editor, considered by many to be a good free alt to Illustrator.

- Starred [Ani(pm) + NekoWatch](https://fmhy.net/video#anime-streaming) in Anime Streaming, both are fast, have nice UIs / features, and seem to be well liked.

- Starred [Fly Stream, dulo, Spacedom & CineLite](https://fmhy.net/video#stream-aggregators) in Streaming, these have custom players, nice UIs, and solid servers.

- Starred [Unsplash](https://fmhy.net/image-tools#stock-images) in Stock Image Section, huge library, high-quality images, no signup required.

- Starred [Fishstrap](https://fmhy.net/gaming-tools#roblox-tools) in Roblox Tools as its similar to Bloxstrap but seems to be better maintained.

- Starred [Tiny Desk Concerts](https://fmhy.net/audio#concerts-live-shows) in Concerts / Live Shows, popular intimate live performance series launched by NPR in 2008, has over 1000+ shows.

- Starred [MixesDB](https://fmhy.net/audio#dj-mixes-livesets) in Concerts / Live Shows, huge, well=maintained database of DJ sets, radio shows and mixes.

- Starred [Sight and Sound](https://fmhy.net/video#recommendation-tools) in Movie Rec Tools, poll conducted [once a decade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sight_and_Sound) since 1952, takes votes from 1,600+ critics, archivists, etc.

- Starred [KatDrama](https://fmhy.net/video#drama-streaming) in Drama Streaming, has nice player, big library and lots of sources.

- Starred [The Fitness Wiki](https://fmhy.net/misc#physical-health) in Physical Health, comprehensive and well-maintained wiki ran by the r/Fitness community.

- Starred [Timer.Coffee](https://fmhy.net/misc#drinks) in Drinks Section, coffee brewing app with guided timers, recipes, calculators & [more](https://i.ibb.co/d0zdgkjw/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/B61pcPe.png).

- Starred [Gresham College](https://fmhy.net/educational#streaming) in Course Streaming, big collection of high quality lectures, covers many topics.

- Starred [Immich](https://fmhy.net/image-tools#offline-galleries) in Offline Image Galleries, popular, works well, backed by FUTO.

- Starred [Pi](https://fmhy.net/developer-tools#coding-harnesses-extensions) in Coding Harnesses, popular terminal coding agent, customizable via extensions, skills, prompts & themes.

- Starred [Tiarap](https://fmhy.net/storage#free-dns-resolvers) in Free DNS Resolvers, seems to work well for people, good uptime + coverage.
