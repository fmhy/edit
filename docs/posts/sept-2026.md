# Wiki Updates

- The sites search engine now has **[Section Filters](https://i.ibb.co/99gXbTyD/image.png)** / [2](https://i.imgur.com/J1csfSu.png) (Gaming, Audio, Reading etc.) which can improve the quality of results in some situations. TY to anarchydr for doing this.

- Added a **[.onion](http://fnp47tywqhwygfkkik3dkgreocdbevjslzs6fw4zbotomcbqeuyahjqd.onion/)** version of FMHY to our backups page. TY to @saber for making this.

- Added a FMHY **[Bookmark Extension](https://github.com/mian196/fmhy-bookmarks-extension)** w/ Auto Updates. TY to @Simon Riley for making this.

- FMHY **[Raycast Search Extension](https://www.raycast.com/akshit_mehta/fmhy-search)** is now available, thank you to its contributors for the idea + doing this for us.

- Added [Query Parameter](https://i.imgur.com/S67dReX.png) / [2](https://i.ibb.co/yFyybyfX/Untitled.png) support (?q=searchquery). Example: `https://fmhy.net/?q=test`. TY to @flower for doing this.

- Re-ordered [Anime Streaming Sites](https://fmhy.net/video#anime-streaming) based on the **[Polls](https://challonge.com/woium3fu/fullscreen)** and conversations that took place during them, thank you to everyone who voted and congrats to all sites that got bumped higher or maintained their high spots. [Before vs After](https://i.ibb.co/JW4dqsH7/Untitled.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/sNqUdG7.png)

- Electronics shopping has become really bloated, so we've decided to split it into some new subsections to help make it easier to navigate. TY to @jochen for the idea. New subsections: [Electronics / Tech](https://fmhy.net/misc#electronics-tech), [Mobile / Phones](https://fmhy.net/misc#mobile-phones), [Cameras](https://fmhy.net/misc#cameras), [PC Hardware / Building](https://fmhy.net/misc#pc-hardware-building), [TV / Monitors](https://fmhy.net/misc#tv-monitors), [Audio Equipment](https://fmhy.net/misc#audio-equipment), [Keyboards / Mice](https://fmhy.net/misc#keyboards-mice), [Gaming Equipment](https://fmhy.net/misc#gaming-equipment)

- Added section for [AI / ML Learning](https://fmhy.net/educational#ai-ml-learning) to Educational.

- Added section for [Military / Conflict History](https://fmhy.net/educational#military-conflict-history) to Edu History. TY to @Yeet for the idea + collecting sites.

- Added section for [Racing / Driving](https://fmhy.net/gaming#racing-driving) to Browser Games.

- Added section for [Random Sites / Discovery](https://fmhy.net/misc#random-sites-discovery) under Indexes.

- Minecraft Tools category has been [thoroughly revised](https://i.ibb.co/svg60Qp2/Untitled.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/7V6rWeo.png). This included reorganizing and condensing some lines to try to help make it easier to navigate. TY to @Myuui, @Flower, @Jul Mik, @Yoshi/MTG, @litekin for helping with this.

- Removed the mutual exclusion between Toggle Starred + Toggle Indexes, both switches can now be enabled at the [same time](https://i.ibb.co/gMHfjG08/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/sqPgOK3.png). TY to Zenith for doing this.

# Stars Added ⭐

- Starred [GoG Revived](https://fmhy.net/gaming#download-games) in Game Downloads, 1:1 archive of gog-games with updates.

- Starred [Dans Guides](https://fmhy.net/misc#indexes) in Indexes as they're nicely-written and have become well liked by many people.

* Starred [downloadeverythingfromeverywhere](https://fmhy.net/video#download-sites) in Video Download Sites, aggregator that scrapes multiple hosts, including some single click ones.

- Starred [raincord](https://fmhy.net/mobile#social-media-apps) in Mobile Social Media Apps, modded discord client for Android & iOS, successor to Kettu, has plugins, good dev team, and seems to be working well for those currently using it.

- Starred [Movy](https://fmhy.net/video#stream-aggregators) in Stream Aggregators, fast streams, high quality UI, one of the better sites to come out in last few months.

- Starred [LuaTools](https://fmhy.net/gaming-tools#steam-epic) in Steam Tools, Steam multi-tool + manifest tool similar to SteamMidra but smoother and doesn't trigger defender.

- Starred [Shfl](https://fmhy.net/audio#curated-recommendations) in Music Recommendations, recommendations from music writers + artists, has big catalogue, guides, thematic lists and [more](https://i.ibb.co/jP4w9jzF/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/129i3Dk.png). 

- Starred [NPR's Books We Love](https://fmhy.net/reading#book-recommendations) in Book Recommendations, [reflects thousands](https://i.imgur.com/px72ucF.png) / [2](https://i.ibb.co/Kxw7L73t/image.png) of hand-picked recommendations from their hundreds of reporters, critics and staff members.

- Starred [Refra + Right Gallery](https://fmhy.net/mobile#image-galleries) in Android Image Galleries, both have very nice UIs, lots of features, and were well liked by those who tried them.

- Starred [Crafty Controller](https://fmhy.net/gaming-tools#hosting-tools) in Minecraft Hosting Tools, cross-platform server controller that works in browser, has file uploads, modpack imports and more.⁠

- Starred [SLSsteam](https://fmhy.net/linux-macos#linux-gaming) in Linux Gaming, Steamclient Mod w/ DLC Unlocker and Emulated Achievements.

- Starred [Together.ai](https://fmhy.net/ai#multiple-model-sites) in Multi Model Sites, has good daily limits, solid free models, and web search.

- Starred [AccuRadio](https://fmhy.net/audio#internet-radio) in Internet Radio, pre-programmed, themed channels spanning hundreds of styles, infinite skips and [more](https://i.ibb.co/6JwVfm8Q/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/ODhkBNk.png).

- Starred [NTS Radio](https://fmhy.net/audio#music-discovery) in Music Discovery, led by a global community of more than [600 resident hosts](https://i.ibb.co/HDYDQWXB/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/8ckHCwU.png), including musicians, DJs, artists, and record collectors, alongside guest shows and special programming. 

- Starred [Rose](https://fmhy.net/gaming-tools#moba-tools) in MOBA Tools, LoL Skin Manager / Unlocker, safe to use, no reports of anyone having any sort of issues, has every single skin in the game (on your screen only).

- Starred [RyukGram](https://fmhy.net/mobile#social-media-apps-1) in iOS Social Media, actively maintained Instagram tweak, works well for those who tried it, seems to be the best current option.

- Starred [8SPINE](https://fmhy.net/mobile#ios-audio) in iOS Audio, module-based streaming + downloading app, has nice UI, lots of settings, and lossless playback (with certain modules).

- Starred [Yomitan](https://fmhy.net/educational#multi-language) in Language Learning, feature-rich browser popup dictionary, has custom themes, anki integration and more.

- Starred [DeepSWE](https://fmhy.net/ai#coding-benchmarks) in Coding Benchmarks, very reliable, frequently updated, and tests all reasoning strengths of models.

- Starred [Cherry Studio](https://fmhy.net/ai#local-ai-frontends) in Local AI Frontends, has a easy setup + nice UI.
