# Wiki Updates

- Re-ordered [Live Sports](https://fmhy.net/video#live-sports) based on the **[polls](https://challonge.com/ajqvt0wo/fullscreen)** and conversations that took place during them. Thank you to everyone who voted and congrats to all sites that got bumped higher, or maintained their high spots.

- Cleaned up the [Interesting Section](https://fmhy.net/misc#interesting), removed any dead/pointless sites, and split some from it into 2 new subsections under Fun: [Media / Lore](https://fmhy.net/misc#media-lore) + [Mysteries / Paranormal](https://fmhy.net/misc#mysteries-paranormal). TY to @jochen for the idea.

- Added section for [Settings / Fixes](https://fmhy.net/gaming-tools#settings-fixes) to Gaming Tools.

- Added section for [Books / Audiobooks](https://fmhy.net/misc#books-audiobooks) to Shopping. TY to @I. Faust for the idea.

- Added section for [Stream Lounges](https://fmhy.net/video#stream-lounges), these are sites that stream or allow users to stream random movies or shows, and generally include chatrooms.

- Added sections for [Desktop Streaming Apps](https://fmhy.net/video#streaming-apps) + [Anime Streaming Apps](https://fmhy.net/video#anime-streaming-apps).

- Added section for [Specialty Download](https://fmhy.net/video#specialty-downloading) under Video Download section.

- Moved the [Survival / Preparedness](https://fmhy.net/educational#survival-preparedness) section out of storage to Edu > Skills + re-sorted & added descriptions.

- Re-organized [Genre Specific Ripping](https://fmhy.net/audio#genre-specific-ripping) by genre popularity (Hip Hop -> Electronic -> Rock/Metal/Punk -> Pop/K-Pop/Disco -> Classical -> Jazz/Fusion -> Bluegrass -> World -> Niche/Experimental). FLAC sites are above MP3. TY to @Myuui for doing this.

- Re-organized [Game Soundtrack](https://i.ibb.co/355Rg75x/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/dbrqrUK.png) section, fixed links, moved sites that fit together to same lines. TY to @Myuui for doing this.

- Re-organized [Audio Tracking / Databases](https://fmhy.net/audio#tracking-databases), combined sites that fit on same line together, tried to make it [easier to navigate](https://i.ibb.co/n83RPsGp/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/ysUDaxy.png). TY to @Myuui for doing this.

- Added Simkl/Trakt/MAL/Anilist/etc integration as a optional way to score on #5 for [stream grading](https://github.com/fmhy/FMHY/wiki/Stream-Site-Grading). Sites won't be punished for not having this, its just now one of the 3 ways (login/code/3rd party) to score here now.

# Stars Added ⭐

- Starred [F-Droid](https://fmhy.net/mobile#foss-apks) in FOSS APKs, one of the most loved and trusted sources for open source apps. Note that Droid-ify (f-droid frontend) is starred in same section, which is why F-Droid itself hadn't been until now.

- Starred [Limusic](https://fmhy.net/audio#streaming-apps) in Audio Streaming Apps, seems to be the cleanest, fastest, and most [feature-rich](https://i.ibb.co/nq0JpBtQ/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/K7n7ddI.png) open-source desktop client for YouTube Music currently.

- Starred [lostnode](https://fmhy.net/video-tools#video-download) in Video Downloaders, supports 600+ sources, has a nice UI + fast downloads.

- Starred [Kryoto](https://fmhy.net/gaming#download-games) in Game Download, big library, fast host(s), unique UI, has become well liked in a short time.

- Starred [Antra Web](https://fmhy.net/audio#audio-ripping-sites) in Audio Ripping, works well, supports many formats and websites.

- Starred [VoiceMeeter](https://fmhy.net/system-tools#system-audio) in System Audio, Well-made virtual audio mixer with Voicemeeter Banana + universal routing for hardware inputs and outputs, aswell as software.

- Starred [Better Lyrics](https://fmhy.net/audio#youtube-music-tools) in YouTube Music Tools, has nice themes, accurate sync, and was well liked by users in the suggestion thread.

- Starred [wBlock](https://fmhy.net/mobile#ios-adblocking) in iOS Adblocking, fast and feature-rich Safari adblocker with custom blocklist support, userscript/userstyle engine and more.

- Starred [LibraryThing Awards](https://fmhy.net/reading#book-recommendations) in Book Recommendations, nice index of book awards & winning books, aggregating major prizes, annual lists and [more](https://i.ibb.co/SXKJ3fJc/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/ZIc5Rfb.png).

- Starred [Retro Online](https://fmhy.net/gaming#retro-browser-emulators) in Retro Browser Emulators, has over 800 games, got a fair amount of positive attention from those who tried it.

- Starred [Autocompressor](https://fmhy.net/video-tools#online-processing) in Video Tools, has free tier with 2 GB file size limit, fast uploads + downloads, and minimal timeouts.

- Starred [Spoti.pw](https://fmhy.net/mobile#ios-audio) as it seems to be the best current option for ad-free spotify on iOS.

- Starred [Vicinae](https://fmhy.net/linux-macos#linux-apps) in Linux Apps, open-source app launcher w/ easy setup, raycast/script compatibility and more.

- Starred [Mocha](https://fmhy.net/file-tools#file-hosts) in File Hosts, 15GB per file, 100GB storage, keeps files 30 days after last download. ⁠

- Starred [DropDrive](https://fmhy.net/file-tools#file-hosts) in File Hosts, 100GB per file, keeps files 30 days after last download.⁠

- Starred [/mental health/](https://fmhy.net/misc#mental-health) in Mental Health section, well researched and put together, a fair amount of people have come forward saying they found it helpful.

- Starred [Vorssaint](https://fmhy.net/linux-macos#system-tools) in macOS System Tools, open-source menu bar toolkit with many features / utilities, including a volume mixer, keyboard shortcuts, screen recording, and more.

- Starred [Pawn Appétit](https://fmhy.net/educational#chess) in Chess Learning, updated and [feature-rich](https://i.ibb.co/v4pkQJsQ/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/2ozAnyF.png) fork of En Crossiant which we previously had starred.

- Starred [OpeningTree](https://fmhy.net/educational#chess) in Chess Learning, pulls a player's match history from Chess.com or Lichess and maps games onto an [interactive board](https://i.ibb.co/LDSnHmx3/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/8Sl7d06.png).

- Starred [PyChess](https://fmhy.net/gaming#chess) in Chess Section, takes Lichess architecture and [expands it](https://i.ibb.co/MD1bTHJq/image.png) / [2](https://i.imgur.com/T2CGAph.png) into a massive, free, open-source museum and battleground for over 60 regional, historical, and fairy chess variants.

- Starred [Accella](https://fmhy.net/linux-macos#linux-gaming) in Linux Gaming, Steam Game + DLC unlocker, nice ui, good dev team, seems to work well for people.⁠

- Starred [ynoTV](https://fmhy.net/video#iptv-players) in IPTV Players, nice UI, feature rich, score/match finder, custom playlists, trakt/simkl integration and more.

- Removed GOG Games as it's now officially offline. Huge thank you and respect to their team for maintaining such a uniquely high-quality site for so long.

- Removed UnTrap (Distraction-Free YouTube Extension) as they seem to of put many useful features behind a paywall.

- Removed all file hosts that have malicious ads. Note that some of these are planning to switch to different ads on Nov 1st, and may be reconsidered at that time if they follow through.

- Removed UnionCrax as they're stepping down.

- Removed MovieParadise as they've closed invites.
